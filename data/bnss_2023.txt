Section 483 of the Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023 provides that when any person accused of a non-bailable offence is arrested, he may be released on bail by the Court after considering the nature of the accusation. The Court shall impose conditions to ensure the presence of the accused during trial. Bail in non-bailable offences is a matter of judicial discretion and not a matter of right.
